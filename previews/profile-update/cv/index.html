Research Experience

AvatarMix: High-Quality 3D Virtual Try-On Doctoral research

Overview. Developed a high-quality 3D virtual try-on method that combines two 3D avatars to show one person wearing the other's clothing while preserving the wearer's face and body shape.

Method. Proposed a method that combines the wearer's head with the clothing donor's avatar and adapts the entire body to the wearer's shape. Used a diffusion model to repair composition seams and appearance artifacts caused by deformation.

Contribution. Formulated the body adaptation and seam repair problems and led method development, implementation, evaluation, visualization, and manuscript preparation.

Publication. Accepted to CVPR 2026 Findings.

Paper Project page Code

EG-HumanNeRF: Fast Human Novel-View Synthesis Master's research

Overview. Developed a feed-forward method that synthesizes novel views of a person from three or four images, balancing rendering speed and image quality without per-person retraining.

Method. Used the SMPL-X human body model and predicted geometry to narrow NeRF sampling regions in two stages, reducing computation. Improved image quality through occlusion-aware image refinement.

Contribution. Developed the two-stage sampling method and occlusion-aware refinement, accelerated the overall pipeline, and led implementation, evaluation, and manuscript preparation.

Publication. Published in Computational Visual Media, 12(2), 355–379 (2026).
